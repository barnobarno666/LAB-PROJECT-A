## Appendix C: Sample Calculation

### Catalyst Mass Determination

- **Basis:** 12 g of catalyst
- **The ratio of $\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 : \text{NiO}$:** $5 : 1$

$$\text{Mass of NiO in catalyst} = \frac{1 \times 12}{6} = 2\text{ g}$$

$$\text{Mass of } \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 \text{ in catalyst} = \frac{4 \times 12}{6} = 10\text{ g}$$
*(Note: Preserved as written in original report; stoichiometrically $\frac{5 \times 12}{6} = 10\text{ g}$)*

**$\text{NiO}$ is formed by the following reactions:**
$$\text{Ni(NO}_3)_2 \cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Ni(NO}_3)_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
$$\text{Ni(NO}_3)_2 + 2\text{KOH} \rightarrow \text{Ni(OH)}_2 + 2\text{KNO}_3$$
$$\text{Ni(OH)}_2 \rightarrow \text{NiO} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$$

$$1\text{ mole NiO} \equiv 1\text{ mole Ni(NO}_3)_2 \cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
$$74.70\text{ g NiO} \equiv 290.80\text{ g Ni(NO}_3)_2 \cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$$

$$\text{The required amount of Ni(NO}_3)_2 \cdot 6\text{H}_2\text{O} = \frac{290.80 \times 2}{74.71} = 7.785\text{ g}$$

**$\text{Al}_2\text{O}_3$ is formed by the following reactions:**
$$\text{Al(NO}_3)_3 \cdot 9\text{H}_2\text{O} \rightarrow \text{Al(NO}_3)_3 + 9\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
$$\text{Al(NO}_3)_3 + 3\text{KOH} \rightarrow \text{Al(OH)}_3 + 3\text{KNO}_3$$
$$2\text{Al(OH)}_3 \rightarrow \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + 3\text{H}_2\text{O}$$

$$2\text{ mole Al(NO}_3)_3 \cdot 9\text{H}_2\text{O} \equiv 1\text{ mole Al}_2\text{O}_3$$
$$750.26\text{ g Al(NO}_3)_3 \cdot 9\text{H}_2\text{O} \equiv 102\text{ g Al}_2\text{O}_3$$

$$\text{The required amount of Al(NO}_3)_3 \cdot 9\text{H}_2\text{O} = \frac{375.13 \times 10 \times 2}{102} = 73.55\text{ g}$$

### Inlet Gas Flow Rates

$$\text{H}_2 = \frac{\frac{10\text{ L}}{\text{hr}} \times 273\text{ K} \times 760\text{ mmHg} \times 1\text{ gmole}}{(273.15 + 32)\text{ K} \times 760\text{ mmHg} \times 22.4\text{ L}} = 0.399\text{ }\frac{\text{gmole}}{\text{hr}}$$

$$\text{N}_2 = \frac{\frac{20\text{ L}}{\text{hr}} \times 273\text{ K} \times 760\text{ mmHg} \times 1\text{ gmole}}{(273.15 + 32)\text{ K} \times 760\text{ mmHg} \times 22.4\text{ L}} = 0.799\text{ }\frac{\text{gmole}}{\text{hr}}$$

### Outlet Gas Flow Rates & Composition

**Outlet gas volumetric flow rate, $V_{tot}$:**
$$V_{tot} = 7.7\text{ }\frac{\text{cm}^3}{\text{min}} \times \frac{60\text{ min}}{1\text{ hr}} \times \frac{1\text{ m}^3}{10^6\text{ cm}^3} = 462 \times 10^{-6}\text{ }\frac{\text{m}^3}{\text{hr}}$$

**Molar flow rate = $n_{tot}$:**
$$\text{We know, } P V_{tot} = n_{tot} R T$$

$$\text{or, } n_{tot} = \frac{P V_{tot}}{R T} = \frac{462 \times 10^{-6} \times 101325 \times 1}{305.15 \times 8.314} = 18.45 \times 10^{-3}\text{ }\frac{\text{gmole}}{\text{hr}}$$

**At $32^\circ\text{C}$, Vapor pressure of water = $35.7\text{ mmHg}$ (NIST WebBook)**
- Dry gas flow rate at NTP = $n_{dry}$
- Since $P \propto n$ (Avogadro’s law),

$$\therefore n_{dry} = \frac{P_{dry} \times n_{tot}}{P_{tot}} = \frac{18.45 \times 10^{-3}\text{ }\frac{\text{gmole}}{\text{hr}} \times (760 - 35.7)\text{ mmHg}}{760\text{ mmHg}} = 17.58 \times 10^{-3}\text{ }\frac{\text{gmole}}{\text{hr}}$$
