Appendix C: Sample Calculation
Catalyst Mass Determination
Basis: 12g of catalyst. 
The ratio of Al2O3: NiO =  5 : 1
Mass of NiO in catalyst = 1 × 126=2 g
Mass of Al2O3 in catalyst =4×126=10 g
NiO is formed by the following reaction:
Ni(NO3)2.6H2O → Ni(NO3)2 + 6H2O
Ni(NO3)2 + 2KOH→ Ni(OH)2+ 2KNO3
Ni(OH)2 → NiO+H2O
 1 mole NiO ≡ 1 mole Ni(NO3)2.6H2O
74.70 g NiO ≡ 290.80 g Ni(NO3)2.6H2O
The required amount of Ni(NO3)2.6H2O = 290.80×274.71=7.785 g
Al2O3 is formed by the following reaction
Al(NO3)3.9H2O → Al(NO3)3 + 9H2O
Al(NO3)3 + 3KOH→ Al(OH)3 + 3KNO3
2Al(OH)3→Al2O3+3H2O
 2 mole Al(NO3)3.9H2O ≡ 1 mole Al2O3
 750.26g Al(NO3)3.9H2O ≡  102g Al2O3
The required amount of Al(NO3)3.9H2O = 375.13×10×2102=73.55 g
