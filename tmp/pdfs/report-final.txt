Here Wcat is catalyst mass and FCO,in is inlet CO molar ﬂow. This ratio has units of catalyst mass per CO

The discrete grid search identiﬁes the best sampled operating point for the Full M4 reactor model. It maximizes
completed-bed CO conversion over four inputs: inlet H2 /CO ratio, inlet temperature, inlet pressure, and catalyst
space time on a CO-feed basis. The Experiment 1 catalyst mass (3.12 g), bed geometry and voidage, and N2 /CO
inlet ratio are ﬁxed. At each selected H2 /CO ratio and space time, the inlet ﬂows are reconstructed as
